About GEMONA

Purpose in every product. Integrity in every partnership.

Established under the laws of the Arab Republic of Egypt, GEMONA is an Egyptian joint stock company serving the agricultural commodities and FMCG sectors through international trading channels and value-add Process, with growing capabilities in construction materials. We bridge international networks with the Egyptian market to build reliable supply chains and lasting value.

The Meaning Behind GEMONA

Inspired by a town that traded, rebuilt, and endured.

GEMONA takes its name from Gemona del Friuli, the historic Italian town known for its strategic role in trade, finance and logistics. Its legacy of commerce, resilience, and rebuilding reflects the values that shape GEMONA today: strategic partnerships, reliability, operational excellence, and the ability to adapt and grow.

Steel Trading & Manufacturing

Supplying steel, contributing to national infrastructure growth.
